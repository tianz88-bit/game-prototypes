Flight controls

Hold W/S to accelerate/brake, A/D to yaw, Space to fire. Arrow keys also work. Tap a button for a short manoeuvre, or hold it. The ship stays centred in this preview.

Thrust dips the nose, braking raises it, yaw banks into the turn. Ordinary heading rotation stays level.

Motion layers

Engine cores vibrate out of phase with a small axial rattle and uneven lateral shake. Cowlings carry only 12% of the vibration. Wings flex subtly. Guns kick back sharply, squash wider/shorter and stretch through their return.
