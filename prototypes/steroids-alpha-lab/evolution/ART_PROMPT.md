# Missile Cruiser source art

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool using the existing Human Nomad ship review and booster cowling as style references. Transparent output; alpha-trimmed individual pieces, then assembled using the shared cardinal-offset rig. No screen capture used.

Source atlas and prepared PNGs are retained in `outputs/steroids-alpha/missile-cruiser-lab/` outside Git. R2 package and exact hashes: `assets.json`. PNGs and ZIPs must not enter Git.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent SPRITE PARTS ATLAS for a NEW Human Nomad MISSILE CRUISER. Image1 is only the style/palette/projection reference; image2 is only the detailed casing material reference. Create eight DISTINCT NEW isolated modular cutouts in a perfectly regular FOUR COLUMN by TWO ROW grid. The entire canvas is transparent, every cell has generous clear padding so parts never touch any cell boundary or each other. No words, labels, borders, shadows or background. Orthographic directly overhead, NO isometric camera, NO perspective vanishing point, nose points straight UP. Like Zelda top-down maps, show a SMALL lower/right edge lip on raised plates while preserving a top-facing planar shape. Bold dark navy outlines, vibrant cobalt blue armor, safety orange vents/braces, cream edge caps, cyan glass, very simple clean painted/cel-shaded planes exactly matching reference ships, moderate detail readable at small game scale. NOT photorealistic, NOT intricate greebles, NOT blurry concept painting. This ship must be new and more elongated than the broad reference battlecruiser, a space missile boat with a central launch bay, no primary gun barrels anywhere. Each cell contains ONLY the named part, no complete ship, all facing straight up. ROW1 COLUMN1: long narrow complete central keel/hull underbody, tapered angular blue bow, dark navy mechanical spine, roughly 2.8 times as tall as wide, no cockpit/guns/engines/bay integrated. ROW1 COLUMN2: one long slim cobalt side armor sponson, angular segmented silhouette, cream raised rim and orange structural braces, about 2.5 times tall as wide; used mirrored in rig. ROW1 COLUMN3: long rectangular central missile bay tray in dark navy recessed metal, EXACTLY EIGHT large unmistakable closed missile cell doors arranged TWO columns and FOUR rows; blue tray casing, cream/orange thick individual rectangular hatch rims, no missiles drawn out, no guns. ROW1 COLUMN4: one separate single rectangular closed missile hatch door seen from overhead, cream chamfered rim, orange middle plate, a simple visible hinge edge. ROW2 COLUMN1: a NEW raised aft bridge pedestal, broad stepped cobalt/cream trapezoidal base, only the base, no glass; not the reference fighter cockpit. ROW2 COLUMN2: a separate low wide cyan bridge visor/glass inset sized to fit the preceding pedestal, dark navy frame and large cyan panes, no base. ROW2 COLUMN3: a standalone compact rectangular rear engine core, dark metal with recessed cyan exhaust mouth at its BOTTOM, a small orange housing, no flame. ROW2 COLUMN4: a standalone cobalt blue engine cowling shell with cream rim and orange top vents, a clear BOTTOM opening into which the preceding core will be fitted, no flame. All eight parts have flat underside and consistent exact-overhead projection, some readable bevel thickness. Keep part atlas functional for alpha-cropping and independently layering/animating. Do not copy or stretch existing hull shapes. No new palettes beyond the reference. Transparent background.
