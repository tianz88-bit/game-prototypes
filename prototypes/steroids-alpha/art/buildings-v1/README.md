# Layered Nexus and dual-cannon tower kit

Seven original, transparent, overhead cel-painted parts generated with the built-in image tool on 16 November 2026. Original PNGs, exact prompts and the source ZIP are in `outputs/steroids-alpha/buildings-rig-v01` in the art workspace. No PNG/ZIP is committed. `assets.json` records every original source hash, alpha frame and runtime budget. Owner approved release on 16 November 2026. The exact ZIP is uploaded to the public R2 URL in the manifest and was re-fetched with matching SHA-256 before release.

The Nexus has a 260-world-unit foundation, 190-unit reactor body lifted 45 units, and a 180-unit crown lifted 100 units. Its foundation fits inside the existing landing-pad inner edge (175 units from the centre). Three independent images supply fixed Z and pronounced parallax rather than a baked whole-building illustration.

The tower uses a 44-unit foot, shaft lifted 20, head lifted 60, and two copies of the same barrel lifted 62, laterally offset by -8/+8. Existing `baseFiredAt` events alternate visual recoil and muzzle flash; there are no additional shots or gameplay changes. Head/barrels follow the projected aim; foundation and shaft remain ground-based. Projected neighbouring world axes control bearing and foreshortening as the camera moves.

Muted cylindrical support sprites connect Nexus levels0/45/100 and tower levels0/20/60. They are baked once per clan and reused. Contact-shadow Images remain at the foundation; directional shadow polygons extrude the footprint away from the sun and project every vertex through the same surface transform. The original code-native Nexus/turret art remains the missing-texture fallback. `BuildingRigs.begin/end` culls existing views; `destroy` releases scene-owned images/graphics. Palette textures are prepared once from independent Crimson/Azure building material ramps; no ship palette remapper is used, and raw source textures are released after preparation.

Boot integration: `queueBuildingArt(scene)`, then `prepareBuildingArt(scene)`. Flight integration: create `BuildingRigs(scene)`; call `begin()` before base drawing and `end()` afterwards, including when there is no layout. Pass it to `drawClanBase` as `rigs`; destroy on rebuild/shutdown. Returned treasury anchors include `labelY` and `top` to clear the larger footprint.
