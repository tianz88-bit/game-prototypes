# Rotation order and transverse proportions

Candidate fists use rotation-dependent depth (front to back):

- Up: complete head, left/right fists.
- Left: complete head, anatomical left fist, right fist.
- Right: complete head, anatomical right fist, left fist.
- Down: left/right fists, complete head.

Face and hair stay grouped; a glove never sorts between them. Intermediate fist depths vary with facing direction. This takes precedence over the old fixed guard depth. Original comparison is unchanged.

Further transverse shape adjustment: torso across-body span 0.86 × rig torso size, front/back depth 0.44 × that span. Abdomen span 0.73 × rig torso size, front/back depth 0.40 × that span. Shoulder attachments track the wider body. Existing assets are rendered with these proportions; the saved tuning file is untouched.
